# Information Technology

## About

My career in information technology spans more than 35 years across enterprise, manufacturing, retail, consulting, and creative environments. Throughout that career, my focus has been less about any single technology and more about how technology supports people, organizations, and the work they need to accomplish.

My experience has centered on project management, people leadership, technology support, customer service, infrastructure, hardware and software selection and implementation, database administration, training, and workflow development. I have worked primarily across Windows and macOS environments, with additional experience supporting Unix and Linux platforms.

Technology has changed considerably throughout my career, but the underlying work has remained remarkably consistent: understanding requirements, evaluating solutions, managing projects, supporting users, developing teams, solving problems, improving processes, and helping organizations successfully adopt new technologies.

That work now includes the evaluation and practical adoption of assistive and generative AI as another evolution in how people create, communicate, research, and work.

### Business Systems & Process

* **Workflow & Process Improvement** — Identifying opportunities to simplify processes, improve service delivery, reduce manual effort, and better align technology with operational requirements.
* **Documentation & Standards** — Developing procedures, technical documentation, operational standards, and user resources that support consistency and organizational knowledge.

### Adoption & Enablement

* **Training & User Enablement** — Developing and delivering training that helps users and staff understand new technologies, systems, processes, and workflows.
* **Technology Adoption** — Helping organizations and individuals move from technology selection and implementation to effective everyday use.
